## 4. Per-Dataset Evaluation Detail

### WELFake (English)

**Local verification performed:** 72,134 four-field records verified via read-only CSV inspection. Label 0: 35,028; label 1: 37,106; 783 empty text fields. All labels are 0 or 1; no parser errors with sufficient field size limit.

**Zenodo declaration:** Record 4561253 declares `Label (0 = fake and 1 = real)` and reports 35,028 real / 37,106 fake. Local counts match Zenodo's reported totals **only under reversal** (local 0 count matches Zenodo's "real" total of 35,028; local 1 count matches Zenodo's "fake" total of 37,106). This numerical alignment is **not** per-row label semantics evidence.

**Paper access:** IEEE Xplore page did not return readable article content. PDF endpoint returned unsupported content type. No full-text access obtained.

**Source provenance:** Zenodo names "Kaggle, McIntire, Reuters, BuzzFeed Political" generically. Three of four source components are conclusively verified: Kaggle source verified as William Lifferth's 2018 competition (20,800 records matching Table III; B-03 closed); McIntire verified as George McIntire's 2016/2017 dataset (6,335 records matching Table III, 6 sample records verified with 100% label concordance; B-04 closed with audit limitations); Reuters verified as ISOT Fake News Dataset (Univ. of Victoria, Ahmed et al. 2018; 44,898 RFC-4180 logical records matching Table III with 0 discrepancy, 44,898 / 44,898 records matched with 100% label concordance; B-05 closed with attribution limitations). For BuzzFeed Political (B-06), candidate source is identified as Benjamin D. Horne & Sibel Adali (2017) fakenewsdata1 (derived from Craig Silverman 2016); Table III reports 101 records (53 Real, 48 Fake) while candidate derivative reports 91 rows (a 10-record difference whose exact relationship has not been established); arithmetic remainder subtraction ($72,134 - 72,033 = 101$) does not prove component exclusivity or source identity; individual record matching is untested (B-06 is PARTIALLY RESOLVED). 72,033 / 72,134 records (99.86%) are accounted for across verified components.

**Rights:** Zenodo declares CC BY 4.0. Investigated in B-07 report (`docs/research/P01_B07_WELFAKE_UPSTREAM_CONTENT_RIGHTS_INVESTIGATION_REPORT.md`): this is deposit-level metadata only. Upstream articles from NYT, Breitbart, and Reuters remain proprietary copyright material; claimed explicit ISOT non-commercial restriction is unverified from primary source; copyright implications depend on jurisdiction and factual use; commercial reuse and raw redistribution scope unresolved (B-07 unresolved).

**Disposition:** ON HOLD. B-01 through B-05 closed with documented evidence and limitations. B-06 is partially resolved (candidate source identified; full record-level provenance unverified). Do not train or demonstrate publicly until B-06, B-07 (upstream rights), and formal mentor approval are resolved.
