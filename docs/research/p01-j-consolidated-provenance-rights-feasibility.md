# VeriNews P01-J — Consolidated Dataset Provenance, Rights & Feasibility Research

**Research date:** 2026-11-07  
**Scope:** Documentation-only public-source review consolidating P01-I consistency, WELFake H-03, English/Hindi alternatives, rights evidence, and current feasibility. No dataset/archive was downloaded, no local raw file was opened, and no audit, preprocessing, training, or evaluation was performed.  
**Project decision:** **Continue research; no dataset selected or approved.** P01 remains **OPEN / AWAITING DATASET EVIDENCE AND MENTOR APPROVAL**. H-01 and H-02 remain **UNRESOLVED**. WELFake remains **ON HOLD**. No dataset is approved for local training or public portfolio demonstration. P02 is **NOT STARTED**.

## 1. Executive decision

No reviewed English or Hindi candidate passes the combined task, label/provenance, exact-release integrity, dataset-terms, upstream-content-rights, evaluation, and mentor-approval gates for VeriNews local training and public demonstration. A public download link, an open repository, an academic paper licence, or a code licence is not by itself a grant for every intended dataset activity.

- **Closest English article-format lead:** ISOT, but its binary classes are assembled by source group (Reuters vs. outlets characterized as unreliable), creating a source-proxy target rather than independently verified article-level truth. Its official download page/README do not state a dataset reuse licence; article rights remain unestablished. It is **CONDITIONAL**, not approved.
- **Closest Hindi article-format lead:** TaLLIP lists a Hindi train/test edition, but its exact release, script/content, labels, annotation, split, and dataset rights are not established. It is **PENDING**, not approved.
- **WELFake:** the deposit confirms only generic source names and a broad merge claim. Upstream identities, contribution counts, label origin, transformation/deduplication procedure, and record lineage are not established for the exact release. H-03 remains **UNRESOLVED**; H-01/H-02 stay unresolved.
- **Hindi archive with the most existing local evidence:** the Indic Zenodo Hindi archive, but the prior audit and paper/record comparison identify substantial release/count/duplicate/length/language discrepancies and a CC BY vs. CC BY-NC declaration conflict. It remains **PENDING / NOT APPROVED**.
- **No alternative candidate is approved** for training, public demonstration, or use as an independent external test in this review.

This report is a research assessment, not legal advice or project authorization. Candidate status terms are project dispositions: **APPROVED** means all project gates passed (none do); **CONDITIONAL** means a plausible task lead with specified blockers; **PENDING** means identity/evidence/rights are insufficient; **REJECTED** means a material task mismatch for the current MVP.

## 3. WELFake H-03 — construction and provenance investigation

### 3.1 Direct primary evidence

The [Zenodo record](https://zenodo.org/records/4561253) and its [API](https://zenodo.org/api/records/4561253) identify the v0.1 record as *WELFake dataset for fake news detection in text data*, DOI `10.5281/zenodo.4561253`, and list the file `WELFake_Dataset.csv`. Its description says:

> “we merged four popular news datasets (i.e. Kaggle, McIntire, Reuters, BuzzFeed Political)”

and states the merge was intended “to prevent over-fitting of classifiers and to provide more text data for better ML training.” It describes a 72,134-article dataset and four columns: serial number, title, text, and label. The API references a BuzzFeed News repository and other corpora/papers, but does not map those references to rows or partitions. The API lists one CSV file; this fact is metadata only—the file was not opened or downloaded.

The original paper is *“WELFake: Word Embedding Over Linguistic Features for Fake News Detection,”* IEEE TCSS 8(4), 881–893 (2021), DOI [10.1109/TCSS.2021.3068519](https://doi.org/10.1109/TCSS.2021.3068519), with publisher record [IEEE Xplore 9395133](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9395133). DOI resolution and [Crossref metadata](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1109/TCSS.2021.3068519) confirm bibliographic details and point to the publisher; they do not expose the construction methods. The IEEE page returned no readable content in the checked fetch route, and the Crossref-listed PDF endpoint was not readable by the available reader. No construction passage, appendix, or supplement was verified in this batch. The related P2C2 paper is a separate work and is not substituted for WELFake-specific evidence.

### 3.3 H-03 conclusion

**H-03 remains UNRESOLVED.** The source pool and merge purpose are only depositor-reported. There is not enough primary, release-linked evidence to establish exact upstream dataset identity/version, per-source contribution, labeling method, transformation/deduplication, or record-level lineage. Documented leakage risks include possible source/label, topic, time, and duplicate confounding because the deposit describes a multi-source merge; these are **plausible risks, not measured leakage findings**. Do not use publisher reputation or dataset names to infer the truth of any article.

### Rights-source distinctions

- **Dataset licence/terms:** Zenodo dataset metadata, CT-FAN terms, AIKosh contributor-selected artefact terms, and IFND's README research-purpose notice are dataset/platform declarations of differing scope. They are not independently verified rights chains.
- **Software licence:** TaLLIP's MIT licence applies to repository “Software” as labelled; it is not established as a dataset-content licence. Other repository software licences likewise do not license news articles.
- **Scholarly paper licence:** ACL Anthology says post-2016 ACL materials are CC BY 4.0; Crossref records the IEEE WELFake article as CC BY 4.0; PMC records the IFND article as CC BY 4.0. Each is publication metadata/terms, not automatic permission for the separate dataset or third-party article content.
- **Upstream article/post rights:** not established for WELFake, ISOT, NELA-GT, TaLLIP, the Indic Hindi archive, IFND, FakeNewsNet, Fakeddit, or BFNK. Platform access and transformed text do not by themselves settle rights in source content.
- **AIKosh platform terms:** the official [terms](https://aikosh.indiaai.gov.in/home/terms-n-conditions) say datasets are available “under specified licenses and end-use terms”; open-access artefacts are downloadable by registered users, while restricted artefacts require contributor consent. They also disclaim verification/endorsement of contributor-generated links and state the platform does not warrant licence compliance. No specific HinFakeNews/BSAkash artefact terms were identified.

**Most constrained source wording:** CT-FAN explicitly limits use to research, permits non-reconstructive summaries/analyses, forbids dataset portions and sharing, and disallows unauthorized reproduction/derivative works/distribution/public display. FakeNewsNet explicitly says the complete dataset cannot be distributed because of Twitter privacy policies and news publisher copyright. IFND says research purposes only. None of these statements makes a dataset suitable for a public VeriNews demo.
